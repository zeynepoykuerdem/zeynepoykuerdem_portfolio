# Blog post template

Use this structure for every post. Keep each section short; a post should take about five minutes to read.

## Title

Short and specific. Say what the post is about, e.g. "Building my site in public: the stack and the first mistakes".

## Cover image

One image, landscape, at least 1200 px wide. Make it in Canva using the same colours and font as the site.

## Giriş (intro)

Two to four sentences. What is this post about, and why does it matter to you now?

## Gelişme (development)

The core of the post. Use short paragraphs. For each step, cover:

- what you did
- what went wrong, if anything
- what you decided and why

## Sonuç (conclusion)

Two to three sentences. What is the main takeaway?

## Next time

One line. Name the topic of the next post. Only promise what you will actually write.

---

Before publishing: check the post in `/blog` on a preview, read it once aloud, and make sure the body keeps its blank lines between paragraphs.
